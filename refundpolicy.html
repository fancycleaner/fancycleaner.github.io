FancyCleaner Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing FancyCleaner. The steps for requesting a refund or canceling a subscription depend on whether you purchased through the Apple App Store or the FancyCleaner website.

Policy for Purchases Made via the Apple App Store

1. Billing and Refunds

Apple handles billing and reviews refund requests for purchases made through the App Store. Refund eligibility depends on Apple's policies and any rights you have under applicable law.

2. How to Request a Refund from Apple

Visit Apple's Report a Problem page and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Choose Request a refund, select the FancyCleaner purchase, and follow the prompts to submit your request.

3. How to Cancel Your App Store Subscription

  • Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name.
  • Tap Subscriptions and select FancyCleaner.
  • Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel before your next renewal to avoid future charges. To avoid being charged after an Apple free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Policy for Purchases Made via Our Website

1. Billing and Refunds

The FancyCleaner support team handles refund requests for purchases made directly on our website. Any refund rights you have under applicable law continue to apply.

2. How to Request a Website Refund

Email fancycleaner.feedback@outlook.com to request a refund. To help us locate your purchase, please include:

  • The email address you used to purchase the subscription.
  • Your order number or transaction ID, if available.
  • A brief description of why you are requesting a refund.

3. How to Cancel Your Website Subscription

  • Sign in to the FancyCleaner website using the email address you used to sign up.
  • Open Subscription in the upper-right corner.
  • Select Cancel Subscription to complete the cancellation.

If you need help canceling, email fancycleaner.feedback@outlook.com. To request a refund for a charge already made, follow the refund steps above.
